An Introduction to Driving: practice questions

An Introduction to Driving

  • Which of the following is illegal while driving?

    • Wearing a headset or ear plugs that covers both ears.
    • Wearing a headset or ear plugs that covers one ear.
    • Using cruise control on residential streets.
    Answer

    Wearing a headset or ear plugs that covers both ears.

    From the DMV's Class C Test #4

  • How does being tired affect your driving?

    • It slows your reaction to hazards.
    • It sharpens your focus for a short while.
    • It matters only late at night.
    Answer

    It slows your reaction to hazards.

Controlling the Vehicle

  • For push/pull steering, where should your hands start on the wheel?

    • At 10 and 2, or 11 and 1 o'clock.
    • At 9 and 3, or 8 and 4 o'clock.
    • Both hands at the top of the wheel.
    Answer

    At 9 and 3, or 8 and 4 o'clock.

  • When turning with push/pull steering, should your hands cross over the middle of the wheel?

    • No, they should never cross.
    • Yes, once for every full turn.
    • Only on sharp, slow turns.
    Answer

    No, they should never cross.

    In push/pull steering your hands never cross the middle of the wheel, and they stay in position even when turning.

  • When should you use hand-over-hand steering?

    • When changing lanes at freeway speed.
    • When backing up and looking behind you.
    • When parking or turning at low speed.
    Answer

    When parking or turning at low speed.

    Use hand-over-hand steering for low-speed turns, parking, or recovering from a skid. When turning while backing, steer with one hand at 12 o'clock.

  • You are turning while backing up and looking behind you. Where may you place one hand on the wheel?

    • At 12 o'clock.
    • At 6 o'clock.
    • At 9 o'clock.
    Answer

    At 12 o'clock.

  • When is it acceptable to steer with one hand while driving forward?

    • When you must work a vehicle control.
    • On long, straight stretches of freeway.
    • Whenever you are below 25 mph.
    Answer

    When you must work a vehicle control.

Signals, Horns, and Headlights

  • When should you use hand-and-arm signals in a car?

    • Only when you are riding a bicycle or motorcycle.
    • When your signal lights don't work or are hard to see.
    • At night, so drivers can see what you plan to do.
    Answer

    When your signal lights don't work or are hard to see.

  • The driver ahead holds an arm straight out of their window. What are they about to do?

    • Turn left.
    • Turn right.
    • Slow down or stop.
    Answer

    Turn left.

    An arm held straight out signals a left turn.

  • The driver ahead holds an arm out of their window, bent up at the elbow. What are they about to do?

    • Turn left.
    • Turn right.
    • Slow down or stop.
    Answer

    Turn right.

    An arm held out and bent up at the elbow signals a right turn.

  • The driver ahead holds an arm out of their window, bent down at the elbow. What are they about to do?

    • Turn left.
    • Turn right.
    • Slow down or stop.
    Answer

    Slow down or stop.

    An arm bent down at the elbow means the driver is slowing down or stopping.

  • A bicyclist ahead holds one arm straight out to the side, pointing. What does it mean?

    • They want you to pass them.
    • They plan to turn that way.
    • They are slowing to stop.
    Answer

    They plan to turn that way.

  • You are about to change lanes on an empty road. What should you do about signaling?

    • Signal anyway before you move over.
    • Skip it; no one is there to see.
    • Signal only if a car comes into view.
    Answer

    Signal anyway before you move over.

  • On a freeway, what is the least time you should signal before changing lanes?

    • Five seconds.
    • Two seconds.
    • Ten seconds.
    Answer

    Five seconds.

  • Should you signal before pulling away from the curb?

    • Yes, before you pull out.
    • Only if a car is coming.
    • No, signals are only for turns.
    Answer

    Yes, before you pull out.

  • You plan to turn into a driveway just past an intersection. When should you signal?

    • When you are almost through the intersection.
    • Just before you enter the intersection.
    • Only once you start to turn into it.
    Answer

    When you are almost through the intersection.

    Signal when you are almost through the intersection, so drivers don't think you are turning at it.

  • On a narrow mountain road, below what sight distance ahead should you sound your horn?

    • 500 feet.
    • 200 feet.
    • 100 feet.
    Answer

    200 feet.

  • When should you use your horn?

    • To tell a slow driver to hurry up.
    • To show another driver you are upset.
    • To warn others of a hazard.
    Answer

    To warn others of a hazard.

  • How many feet away should you switch from high beam to low beam headlights when approaching a vehicle coming towards you?

    • 900 feet.
    • 700 feet.
    • 500 feet.
    Answer

    500 feet.

    From the DMV's Class C Test #1

  • Which lights should be used if a driver is having trouble seeing other vehicles because of dust or smoke blowing across the roadway?

    • Parking lights.
    • Emergency lights.
    • Headlights.
    Answer

    Headlights.

    From the DMV's Class C Test #4

  • What is the closest you may follow another vehicle with your high beams on?

    • 500 feet.
    • 100 feet.
    • 300 feet.
    Answer

    300 feet.

  • What is the rule for driving with only your parking lights on?

    • It is never allowed.
    • It is allowed until 30 minutes after sunset.
    • It is allowed in light rain.
    Answer

    It is never allowed.

    Driving with only your parking lights on is illegal at any time. In rain, and from 30 minutes after sunset, use your headlights.

  • You must use your headlights when it is too dark to see how far ahead?

    • 500 feet.
    • 100 feet.
    • 1,000 feet.
    Answer

    1,000 feet.

  • What is the latest after sunset you may turn on your headlights?

    • 30 minutes.
    • 15 minutes.
    • One hour.
    Answer

    30 minutes.

  • On a sunny day, where should you still use your headlights?

    • Only where a sign says headlights must be on.
    • In tunnels and on mountain roads.
    • Only when you need your wipers.
    Answer

    In tunnels and on mountain roads.

  • You turn on your wipers because of rain during the day. What else must you do?

    • Turn on your high-beam headlights.
    • Turn on your low-beam headlights.
    • Nothing more, since it is daytime.
    Answer

    Turn on your low-beam headlights.

  • You see a crash ahead and need to warn drivers behind you. What can you do?

    • Switch on your high beams.
    • Tap your brakes three or four times.
    • Brake hard so they notice your lights.
    Answer

    Tap your brakes three or four times.

    Warn drivers behind you by tapping your brakes three or four times, turning on your flashers, or using a hand signal as you slow.

  • You must stop for car trouble, and your car has no emergency flashers. How can you warn others?

    • Leave your high beams on.
    • Use your turn signals.
    • Turn on your interior light.
    Answer

    Use your turn signals.

    Turn on your emergency flashers, or your turn signals if the car doesn't have flashers.

  • Your car is failing and you can't get it fully off the road. Where should you stop?

    • Where drivers behind can see you.
    • Just past the top of a hill.
    • Just around a curve, out of sight.
    Answer

    Where drivers behind can see you.
