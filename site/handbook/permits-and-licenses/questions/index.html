Section 2

Getting an Instruction Permit and Driver’s License: practice questions

Applying for an Instruction Permit

  • To get a Class C instruction permit, which tests must you pass?

    • A behind-the-wheel drive test.
    • The knowledge test, and no others.
    • The knowledge and vision tests.
    Answer

    The knowledge and vision tests.

    You must pass the knowledge test and a vision test to get a permit. The drive test comes later, for your license.

  • If you are under 18, what is the youngest age at which you may apply for an instruction permit?

    • 15½.
    • 15.
    • 16.
    Answer

    15½.

    Minors must be at least 15½ years old to apply for an instruction permit, and at least 16 to get a license.

  • As a minor, what must you complete before you can get an instruction permit?

    • 50 hours of supervised practice.
    • A behind-the-wheel drive test.
    • A driver education program.
    Answer

    A driver education program.

    Minors must complete driver education to get a permit. The 50 hours of practice and the drive test come later, before the license.

  • By signing a minor's permit application, what does the parent or guardian accept?

    • A duty to ride along on every single drive.
    • Financial responsibility for the minor.
    • The cost of driver training lessons.
    Answer

    Financial responsibility for the minor.

    A parent or guardian signing the application approves it and accepts financial responsibility for the minor.

  • You are 15½ and just got your instruction permit. When may you start using it?

    • Right away, with any licensed adult.
    • Once instructor training begins.
    • Once your driver education class ends.
    Answer

    Once instructor training begins.

    Minors may not use a permit until they start behind-the-wheel training with an instructor, who validates it.

  • Your parents share joint custody of you. Who must sign your permit application?

    • Either parent.
    • Both parents.
    • The parent you live with most.
    Answer

    Both parents.

Applying for a Driver’s License

  • As a minor with an instruction permit, who may supervise your practice driving?

    • A California-licensed driver 25 or older.
    • Any California-licensed driver 18 or older.
    • Any adult 21 or older, licensed or not.
    Answer

    A California-licensed driver 25 or older.

  • You are 20 and have an instruction permit. What is the youngest the licensed driver with you may be?

    • 21 years old.
    • 18 years old.
    • 25 years old.
    Answer

    18 years old.

    Adults with a permit must drive with a California-licensed driver at least 18. The 25-year minimum applies to minors.

  • Where must the licensed driver supervising a permit holder sit?

    • Anywhere in the car, front or back.
    • Close enough to take control.
    • In the back seat, to watch the road.
    Answer

    Close enough to take control.

  • If you are under 18, what is the youngest age at which you may get a driver's license?

    • 15½.
    • 16.
    • 17.
    Answer

    16.

    Minors must be at least 16 to get a driver's license.

  • As a minor, what is the shortest time you must have held your instruction permit before scheduling the drive test?

    • 3 months.
    • 6 months.
    • 12 months.
    Answer

    6 months.

  • What is the least supervised practice a minor must complete before getting a license?

    • 30 hours.
    • 100 hours.
    • 50 hours.
    Answer

    50 hours.

    Minors must practice at least 50 hours with a California-licensed driver 25 or older, including 10 hours at night.

  • As a minor, what must you prove to get your driver's license?

    • Driver education, but not driver training.
    • Driver education and driver training.
    • Driver training and your own insurance policy.
    Answer

    Driver education and driver training.

  • Of a minor's 50 required practice hours, what is the least that must be at night?

    • 5 hours.
    • 20 hours.
    • 10 hours.
    Answer

    10 hours.

    At least 10 of the 50 practice hours must be driven at night.

Minor’s Restrictions and Exceptions

  • You are under 18 years of age and have had your license for eight months. You may drive:

    • At any time.
    • Between 5 a.m. and 11 p.m.
    • Between 7 a.m. and 8 p.m.
    Answer

    Between 5 a.m. and 11 p.m.

    From the DMV's Class C Test #2

  • As a provisional driver, how long are you barred from driving between 11 p.m. and 5 a.m.?

    • For the first 6 months.
    • For the first 12 months.
    • Until your 18th birthday.
    Answer

    For the first 12 months.

    Provisional drivers can't drive between 11 p.m. and 5 a.m. during their first 12 months with a license, unless an exception applies.

  • You are 17 with a provisional license. May you drive your 19-year-old friend to the mall?

    • Yes, since your friend is over 18.
    • Yes, as long as it is during the day.
    • Only with a parent or a driver 25+ along.
    Answer

    Only with a parent or a driver 25+ along.

    Provisional drivers can't carry passengers under 20 unless a parent or guardian, or another licensed driver at least 25, is with them.

  • In your first year with a provisional license, you must drive home from work after 11 p.m. What must you carry?

    • A note signed by your parent.
    • Nothing; work trips are always allowed.
    • A note signed by your employer.
    Answer

    A note signed by your employer.

    To be excused for work, a provisional driver must carry a note signed by the employer confirming the job.

  • To drive for a school activity under the exception, a provisional driver needs a note signed by:

    • The parent of each passenger.
    • The principal, dean, or designee.
    • A DMV examiner at your local office.
    Answer

    The principal, dean, or designee.

  • A provisional driver must drive for a medical need with no other way to travel. What must they carry?

    • A note signed by a parent.
    • Nothing, if the need is truly urgent.
    • A note signed by a physician.
    Answer

    A note signed by a physician.

  • To drive an immediate family member under the exception, a provisional driver needs a note signed by:

    • A parent or legal guardian.
    • The family member's doctor.
    • The principal of your school.
    Answer

    A parent or legal guardian.

  • Can a parent or guardian cancel their teen's driver's license?

    • Yes, by filing a cancellation form.
    • No, only the DMV can cancel a license.
    • Only after the teen gets a ticket.
    Answer

    Yes, by filing a cancellation form.
